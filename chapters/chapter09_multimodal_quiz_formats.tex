\chapter{Multi-Modal Quiz Formats: Visual, Audio, Video, and Fill-in-the-Blank}

\begin{tcolorbox}[storybox, title={The Story of Nila's Engaging Multimedia Round}]
Nila, a 9th-grade student in Rajshahi, previously found standard 60-question text quizzes monotonous. In previous rounds, telemetry showed her quitting around question 22 after 58 seconds—suffering from standard Multiple-Choice Question (MCQ) fatigue.

Today, Nila launches a tournament round powered by Quizard's **Multi-Modal Engine**:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Question 1 (Image Identification):} A high-resolution picture of the *Somapura Mahavihara* ancient Buddhist monastery appears instantly with 4 historical era options.
    \item \textbf{Question 15 (Audio Snippet):} A 3-second audio snippet plays the signature call of the *Doel (Oriental Magpie-Robin)*—Bangladesh's national bird.
    \item \textbf{Question 30 (Video Clip):} A 4-second video clip shows the winning goal of the 2022 SAFF Women's Championship, asking for the goal scorer.
    \item \textbf{Question 45 (Fill-in-the-Blank):} An interactive Bengali letter-tile keyboard prompts her to complete the missing letters of an ancient rivers name.
\end{enumerate}
Engaged by the rich sensory variety, Nila completes all 60 questions, scoring 52 correct in 140 seconds without experiencing mid-round abandonment.
\end{tcolorbox}

\section{The Format Fatigue Diagnosis (Query H)}

\section{The 4 Core Multi-Modal Question Archetypes}

\subsection{1. Image-Based Identification (\texttt{image\_mcq})}
Presents compressed WebP imagery optimized for mobile networks ($< 45\text{ KB}$ per image). Used for geography, anatomy, science diagrams, and historical landmarks.

\subsection{2. Audio Snippet Challenges (\texttt{audio\_clip})}
Streams lightweight AAC audio files ($< 25\text{ KB}$, $3-5\text{ seconds}$). Used for language pronunciation, musical instruments, historic radio broadcasts, and wildlife identification.

\subsection{3. Short Video Moments (\texttt{video\_clip})}
Delivers ultra-short MP4/AV1 looping clips ($< 250\text{ KB}$, $3-4\text{ seconds}$). Used for sports highlights, physics simulations, and chemistry reactions.

\subsection{4. Fill-in-the-Blank with Virtual Tile Keyboard (\texttt{fill\_blank})}
Contestants type or select jumbled Bengali/English alphabet tiles to spell the correct answer. This eliminates simple guessing and rewards genuine knowledge mastery.

\begin{tcolorbox}[summarybox, title={Chapter Key Takeaways}]
\begin{itemize}
    \item **Direct Solution to Mid-Round Dropouts:** Visual, audio, and tile-puzzle variety prevents the severe drop-off observed at question 20 in text-only rounds.
    \item **Mobile-Optimized Assets:** WebP and AAC compression ($< 50\text{ KB}$) ensures flawless playability across 3G/4G telco networks.
    \item **Client-Side Blob Preloading:** Zero buffering ensures questions render within 5 milliseconds of transition.
\end{itemize}
