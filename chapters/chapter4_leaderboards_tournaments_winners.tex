\begin{tcolorbox}[storybox, title={The Tournament Engine in Brief}]
Quizard's competitive ecosystem operates across two complementary timescales: the **Real-Time Daily Leaderboard** (which updates instantly as players submit scores) and the **Automated Tournament Consolidation Crons** (which verify subscriber billing, freeze official winners, and distribute cash prizes at 4:30 AM and 5:30 AM every week).

\section{The Real-Time Daily Leaderboard Engine}
Throughout the day, thousands of players compete across Bangladesh. When any contestant opens the leaderboard screen, the backend executes the high-performance query (or stored procedure \texttt{sp\_quiz\_leaderboard\_new}):

\begin{lstlisting}[language=sql, caption={The PostgreSQL Leaderboard Window Aggregation Query}]
WITH RankedPlays AS (
    SELECT 
        msisdn, 
        right_count AS score, 
        time_taken, 
        event_id, 
        portal_id, 
        user_id,
        ROW_NUMBER() OVER (
            PARTITION BY msisdn 
            ORDER BY right_count DESC, time_taken ASC, id ASC
        ) AS rn
    FROM quizapp_quizplaysummery
    WHERE is_valid = TRUE 
      AND (date = (CURRENT_TIMESTAMP AT TIME ZONE 'Asia/Dhaka')::date)
      AND (msisdn NOT LIKE '%1798533533%')
      AND (:eventId = 0 OR event_id = :eventId)
      AND (:portalId = 0 OR portal_id = :portalId)
)
SELECT 
    msisdn, 
    score, 
    time_taken,
    DENSE_RANK() OVER (
        ORDER BY score DESC, time_taken ASC
    ) AS user_rank
FROM RankedPlays
WHERE rn = 1
ORDER BY user_rank ASC
LIMIT 100;
\end{lstlisting}

\subsection{Strict Tie-Breaking Hierarchy}
When two or more players achieve high scores, Quizard resolves rank positions using a transparent 3-tier hierarchy:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Primary Criterion (Highest Score):} The player with more correct answers ranks higher.
    \item \textbf{Secondary Criterion (Fastest Time):} If scores are tied (e.g., both Rahim and Karim scored 58/60), the player who finished in fewer seconds ranks higher (Rahim in 140s vs. Karim in 165s $\implies$ Rahim takes Rank 2, Karim takes Rank 3).
    \item \textbf{Tertiary Criterion (Earliest Submission):} If both score and time are identical, the player whose play completed first receives the higher rank.
\end{enumerate}

\section{The Weekly Tournament Championship (Thursday 05:30 AM)}
While daily quizzes offer rapid excitement, the premier championship in Quizard is the **Weekly Mega Tournament**. 

\subsection{The 7-Day Rolling Tournament Cycle}
The tournament week begins at **00:00:00 on Thursday** and concludes at **23:59:59 on Wednesday**. Every Thursday morning at **05:30 AM**, the weekly consolidation cron initiates:
\begin{center}
    \texttt{GET /money/processCronWeeklyWinnerAPIView}
\end{center}

The backend invokes the stored procedure:
\begin{center}
    \texttt{process\_quizard\_top\_winner\_by\_date\_range\_july(event\_id, start\_date, end\_date)}
\end{center}

\subsection{Stored Procedure Architecture}
The stored procedure consolidates all valid plays across the 7-day window, filters out non-subscribers, assigns official serial numbers, and writes the frozen champions to \texttt{quizapp\_weeklywinnerhistorytemp}:

\begin{lstlisting}[language=sql, caption={Weekly Tournament Winner Processing Stored Procedure}]
-- Consolidate 7-day tournament winners and freeze rankings
INSERT INTO quizapp_weeklywinnerhistorytemp (
    msisdn, payer, event_id, service_type, category,
    right_count, time_taken, serial_no, user_id,
    status, week_start_date, week_end_date, portal_id, created_at
)
SELECT 
    q.msisdn,
    s.payer,
    q.event_id,
    'WeeklyTournament' AS service_type,
    'Rapid60' AS category,
    q.right_count,
    q.time_taken,
    ROW_NUMBER() OVER (ORDER BY q.right_count DESC, q.time_taken ASC) AS serial_no,
    q.user_id,
    1 AS status,
    v_start_date AS week_start_date,
    v_end_date AS week_end_date,
    q.portal_id,
    NOW() AS created_at
FROM quizapp_quizplaysummery q
JOIN subscription_subscriptions s ON q.msisdn = s.msisdn
WHERE q.event_id = in_event_id
  AND q.date BETWEEN v_start_date AND v_end_date
  AND q.is_valid = TRUE
  AND s.paymentStatus = 'SUCCEEDED_PAYMENT'
ORDER BY q.right_count DESC, q.time_taken ASC
LIMIT 50;
\end{lstlisting}

\section{The Player Journey: Ayesha Wins the Weekly Championship}
In Rajshahi, Ayesha has been playing consistently throughout the 7-day tournament cycle on Portal 21 (Robi Quizard). 

On Wednesday night at 11:30 PM, Ayesha plays her final allowed round. She achieves a score of **59 out of 60** in **135 seconds**.

\begin{enumerate}
    \item At 05:30 AM on Thursday, the weekly cron runs.
    \item The stored procedure scans the 7-day window ($N=42,000\text{ total plays}$).
    \item Ayesha's active subscription status is verified against billing records.
    \item She is crowned **Serial No. 1 Weekly Champion** with a prize of 25,000 BDT!
    \item When Ayesha opens her app on Thursday morning, \texttt{GET /money/tWinnerApiFrontend} displays her name and profile at the very top of the Hall of Champions.
\end{enumerate}

\begin{tcolorbox}[summarybox, title={Chapter Key Takeaways}]
\begin{itemize}
    \item Live leaderboards use SQL window partitioning (\texttt{ROW\_NUMBER() OVER}) to ensure each player's top round is represented accurately without Frankenstein metric mixing.
    \item Overnight crons at 04:30 AM and Thursday 05:30 AM verify paid subscriber status, preventing unbilled or fraudulent accounts from claiming official prize money.
    \item Frozen winner tables ensure immutable audit records for finance disbursement and regulatory compliance.
\end{itemize}
\end{tcolorbox}
