\chapter{User Personalization Engine, Rank Velocity, and Retention Nudges}

\begin{tcolorbox}[storybox, title={The Story of Tanvir's Personalized Comeback}]
Tanvir, a university student in Sylhet, opened Quizard, played 4 questions in the tournament, got stuck at the free-trial boundary, and stopped. Like hundreds of players in the 15-day dataset (where only 18 players returned for 8+ days), Tanvir was about to become another 1-day churn statistic.

The next day, Tanvir receives a targeted in-app personalization card:
\begin{center}
\textit{``Tanvir, you answered 100\% of your attempted questions yesterday! Unsubscribed players miss out on the 25,000 BDT Weekly Cash Pool. Activate bKash 2 BDT pass to unlock unlimited tournament plays.''}
\end{center}

Intrigued, Tanvir subscribes. His accuracy jumps, and as he climbs the tournament ladder, a glowing $\boldsymbol{\uparrow 8}$ rank-velocity indicator appears on his leaderboard card with the banner: \textit{``You are only 3 points away from the Top 10 prize zone!''} 

By pairing data-backed personalization nudges with visceral rank velocity feedback, Quizard converts a churn-risk user into a daily active contender.
\end{tcolorbox}

\section{The Retention Crisis and Empirical Evidence}
A deep analysis of 15 days of production gameplay telemetry revealed two critical behavioral truths:

\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{The 1-Day Churn Cliff (Query F):} The vast majority of participants play on exactly \textbf{1 day} and never return. Only 18 players maintained an 8-day active streak.
    \item \textbf{The Subscription Engagement Multiplier (Query A):} Subscribed players answer **3x more questions correctly** ($11.42$ vs $1.54$) and attempt **5.6x more questions per session** ($22.81$ vs $4.06$) compared to non-subscribed players. Non-subscribed users hit the 4-question trial wall and abandon the session.
    \item \textbf{The Single-Game Silo (Query C):} Only **3.4\%** of active contestants have played all 4 platform genres. Over 96\% only ever touch a single game (primarily Tournament Event 150).
\end{enumerate}

\section{The Multi-Tier Profile Personalization Engine}
To eliminate the 1-day churn cliff, the backend personalizes the player's home feed and profile cards based on their behavioral cluster:

\subsection{1. Missing Rewards Nudge (Unsubscribed Players)}
For players who achieve $\ge 50\%$ accuracy during free-tier rounds but have not subscribed:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Trigger Condition:} \texttt{is\_subscribed == False} AND \texttt{attempt\_count >= 1} AND \texttt{right\_ratio >= 0.50}.
    \item \textbf{Message Copy:} \textit{``You scored [X]/[Y]! Subscribed players earn real bKash payouts for qualifying scores. Subscribe now for 2 BDT/day to enter the official prize draw.''}
\end{itemize}

\subsection{2. Cross-Game Ecosystem Discovery Nudge (Single-Game Players)}
For contestants who have only ever played one game (e.g., Tournament 150):
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Trigger Condition:} \texttt{distinct\_games\_played == 1}.
    \item \textbf{Message Copy:} \textit{``You are a Tournament specialist! Did you know? Players who also compete in BCS Mind Masters and Wordly earn 2x bonus XP today.''}
\end{itemize}

\subsection{3. Inactivity Win-Back Nudge (Lapsed 24h+ Players)}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Trigger Condition:} \texttt{days\_inactive >= 1} AND \texttt{streak\_count >= 3}.
    \item \textbf{Message Copy:} \textit{``Your [N]-day streak is at risk! Complete 1 round before 11:59 PM to protect your progress towards the Day 15 (+500 XP) surge.''}
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Velocity and Points-to-Top-10 Mathematics}
The backend computes rank delta by comparing the player's current dense rank against their rank snapshot recorded at 12:00 AM:
\begin{equation}
\Delta \text{Rank} = \text{Rank}_{\text{yesterday}} - \text{Rank}_{\text{current}}
\end{equation}
\begin{itemize}
    \item If $\Delta \text{Rank} > 0$: Display green $\boldsymbol{\uparrow \Delta\text{Rank}}$ (Surging upward).
    \item If $\Delta \text{Rank} < 0$: Display red $\boldsymbol{\downarrow |\Delta\text{Rank}|}$ (Slipping downward).
    \item If $\Delta \text{Rank} = 0$: Display gray $=$ (Holding position).
\end{itemize}

The **Points to Top 10 Gap** ($S_{\text{gap}}$) calculates the minimum incremental score needed to enter the prize-eligible Top 10 cutoff:
\begin{equation}
S_{\text{gap}} = \max\left(1, \, S_{\text{rank 10}} - S_{\text{player}} + 1\right)
\end{equation}

-- Daily Rank Snapshots Table for Fast Velocity Lookups
CREATE TABLE leaderboard_daily_snapshots (
    id BIGSERIAL PRIMARY KEY,
    msisdn VARCHAR(15) NOT NULL,
    portal_id INT NOT NULL DEFAULT 15,
    event_id INT NOT NULL,
    snapshot_date DATE NOT NULL DEFAULT CURRENT_DATE,
    snapshot_rank INT NOT NULL,
    best_score INT NOT NULL,
    created_at TIMESTAMP WITH TIME ZONE DEFAULT CURRENT_TIMESTAMP
);
CREATE UNIQUE INDEX idx_rank_snapshot ON leaderboard_daily_snapshots (msisdn, portal_id, event_id, snapshot_date);
\end{lstlisting}
