\chapter{The Financial and Analytics Hub: Reporting Engine, Payouts, and Business Intelligence}

\begin{tcolorbox}[storybox, title={The Reporting Engine in Brief}]
Transparency and financial precision are essential for operating a national real-money tournament platform. Quizard features an enterprise Reporting and Business Intelligence (BI) engine (\texttt{apps.disbursmentReport}, \texttt{apps.allReportGraph}, \texttt{reporting\_app}) that consolidates daily player rankings, validates telco subscriber payments, triggers automated bKash B2C cash disbursements, and renders real-time revenue and retention analytics.
\end{tcolorbox}

\section{The Architecture of the Reporting Engine}
The Reporting Engine unifies data from live gameplay (\texttt{quizapp\_quizplaysummery}), carrier billing logs (\texttt{subscription\_subscriptions}), and payment webhooks into actionable operational reports and automated financial payout workflows.

\section{Core Modules of the Reporting Suite}

\subsection{1. Daily and Weekly Tournament Winner Reports}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Daily Winner Report (\texttt{DailyWinnerViewSet}, \texttt{/report/dailyWinner.html}):} Lists verified top scorers from yesterday, cross-referenced with successful carrier billing status (\texttt{paymentStatus = 'SUCCEEDED\_PAYMENT'}).
    \item \textbf{Weekly Mega Championship Report (\texttt{WeeklyWinnerViewSet}, \texttt{/report/weeklyWinner.html}):} Audits the 7-day tournament leaderboard across all active rules (\texttt{Event 34}, \texttt{Event 150}) with customizable minimum payment count filters (\texttt{payment\_count $\ge 3$}).
\end{itemize}

\subsection{2. Micro-Prize Tier Reporting (5 TK and 10 TK Bounties)}
To reward broad participation beyond the top 3 grand champions, Quizard executes micro-prize reward tiers:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{5 TK Winner Suite (\texttt{5tkWinner}, \texttt{FiveTkReport}):} Stored procedures identify runners-up who achieve threshold scores (e.g., $\text{Score} \ge 25$) within specific rank brackets (e.g., Ranks 4 to 100).
    \item \textbf{10 TK Winner Suite (\texttt{10tkWinner}, \texttt{TenTkWinnerSummaryViewSet}):} Consolidates high-accuracy players eligible for 10 BDT wallet top-ups.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{3. Wordly Word Puzzle Winner Analytics}
In \texttt{apps.disbursmentReport}, dedicated endpoints (\texttt{WordlyDailyWinnerViewSet}, \texttt{WordlyDailyDisbursmentViews}, \texttt{WWeeklyWinner}) audit Bengali and English daily word puzzle accuracy streaks, ensuring Wordly champions are verified before prize payout.

\subsection{4. Referral Tracking and Bounties (\texttt{PrizeOnReferral})}
When existing contestants invite peers using referral codes:
\begin{itemize}
    \item The referral engine tracks successful onboarding through \texttt{report\_referhistory}.
    \item \texttt{PrizeOnReferral} generates automated referral bonus reports, crediting cash bounties once the referee completes their first paid tournament attempt.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{5. Churn-Back and Win-Back Analytics (\texttt{ChurnBackReportViewSet})}
Analyzes lapsed subscribers re-engaging with the platform, calculating promotional return-on-investment (ROI) through stored procedure \texttt{ChurnBackCashBack}.

\subsection{6. Real-Time Revenue and Charging Graphs (\texttt{apps.allReportGraph})}
Powers administrative visual analytics through \texttt{graphDataApi}:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Daily Revenue Trends (\texttt{DailyCharging}):} Real-time revenue graphs tracking carrier billing across bKash, Banglalink, Grameenphone, and Robi.
    \item \textbf{Paid Subscriber Growth (\texttt{NewlyJoinedPaidUser}):} Funnel analysis of new registrations converting to paying subscribers.
    \item \textbf{DAU and Play Volume:} Hourly volume of tournament rounds played nationwide.
\end{itemize}

\section{Real-World Scenario: Tanima Audits and Executes Weekly Payouts}
In Dhaka, Tanima is the Head of Financial Operations at Digital For Good. Every Thursday morning at 09:30 AM, she oversees the national tournament prize disbursement:

\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Leaderboard Verification:} Tanima opens \texttt{/report/weeklyWinner.html}. She reviews the frozen winner roster generated by the 05:30 AM weekly cron:
    \begin{itemize}
        \item \textbf{Rank 1:} Ayesha in Rajshahi (59/60 in 135s, 7 successful billing cycles) $\implies$ \textbf{25,000 BDT}.
        \item \textbf{Rank 2:} Salma in Sylhet (59/60 in 150s, 7 successful billing cycles) $\implies$ \textbf{10,000 BDT}.
        \item \textbf{Rank 3:} Rahim in Dhaka (58/60 in 140s, 7 successful billing cycles) $\implies$ \textbf{5,000 BDT}.
        \item \textbf{Ranks 4--50:} Micro-prize recipients (5 TK and 10 TK bonuses).
    \end{itemize}
    
    \item \textbf{Automated bKash Batch Disbursement:} With one click, Tanima authorizes the payout batch. The backend connects directly to the bKash Merchant B2C Disbursement API:
    \begin{lstlisting}[language=python, caption={bKash B2C Merchant Payout Request Structure}]
payload = {
    "merchantInvoiceNumber": f"QUIZARD-WEEKLY-{event_id}-{winner.id}",
    "receiverMSISDN": winner.msisdn,
    "amount": winner.prize_amount,
    "currency": "BDT",
    "intent": "tournament_prize_disbursement"
}
response = requests.post(f"{BKASH_API_URL}/b2cPayment", json=payload, headers=auth_headers)
    \end{lstlisting}

    \item \textbf{Instant Confirmation:} Within seconds, bKash debits the merchant disbursement account and credits Ayesha's, Salma's, and Rahim's mobile wallets. Automated SMS notifications confirm the payouts, and immutable transaction IDs are recorded in \texttt{UserMigrationAudit}.
\end{enumerate}
